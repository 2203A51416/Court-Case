About Court Case App

The Court Case App is designed to bridge the gap between citizens and legal professionals. Our mission is to provide transparent and timely updates on court cases, ensuring that citizens are well-informed about their legal matters.

With features like lawyer search by specialization, direct case updates, and secure dashboards, we aim to make the legal process more accessible and understandable for everyone.

Whether you're a citizen seeking legal assistance or a lawyer looking to connect with clients, the Court Case App is here to support you every step of the way.

Key Features

  • Lawyer Directory: Search and connect with qualified legal professionals by specialization
  • Case Tracking: Real-time updates and notifications about your court hearings
  • Secure Dashboard: Manage your cases and documents in a private, encrypted space
  • Legal Resources: Access helpful guides and information about the legal process
  • Communication: Direct messaging with your lawyer and case updates
